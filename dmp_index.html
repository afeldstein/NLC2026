Digital Master Plan: Teaching and Learning Ecosystem

North star: Supporting human connection in digital spaces, because meaningful learning happens through relationships.

Learning requires three simultaneous conditions: belonging, connection, and agency.

These are made possible by a teaching and learning ecosystem with five layers, each enabling the next:

  1. Lasting commitment: sustained support for people, time, and systems. Evidence test: Is this work funded and staffed as infrastructure, or sustained by individual effort?
  2. Investing in teaching: so this work is valued, not added on top of everything else. Evidence test: Is faculty participation in professional learning growing? Is that work recognized in workload?
  3. Design for belonging: so no student is invisible in a digital environment. Evidence test: Who hasn't logged in? Who posts without response? Who stopped watching?
  4. Students as partners: agency develops when the environment supports it. Evidence test: Are students seeking support or waiting to be found? Making informed choices about how they learn?
  5. Programs lead change: where faculty shape this work around what their discipline demands. Evidence test: Are programs using data to inform redesign? Are faculty leading this within their disciplines?

Two sustaining conditions run through every layer: trust and transparency in decisions and data, and purposeful tools where connection comes first and the tool comes second.

This is an iterative process, not a linear one. What we learn at each layer reshapes what we build. Evidence flows back up through the system and informs the next cycle of investment, design, and practice.

AI is reshaping every layer of this ecosystem. In courses and programs it affects analytics, feedback, and curriculum, requiring faculty judgment. In student learning it affects writing, research, and problem-solving, requiring student agency. We are building this expertise together through shared inquiry, not mandates.

All of this flows back to the learner: what students are able to think, do, and become.

Read top to bottom. Learning sits at the center because it is the purpose. Belonging, connection, and agency are the conditions learning requires. The teaching and learning ecosystem is what makes those conditions possible — a chain of dependencies where lasting commitment enables investment in teaching, which enables design for belonging, which enables student partnership, which converges in programs where faculty shape the work around what their discipline demands.
