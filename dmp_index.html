How to read this visualization. The center holds the north star of the Digital Master Plan: supporting human connection in digital spaces. The seven themes radiate outward in purple, each representing a dimension of that commitment. Coral action steps orbit at the outer ring, connected by solid lines to their primary theme and dashed lines to the secondary themes they serve. Click any node to explore its narrative and connections.

The dashed orange arcs between themes represent cross-cutting commitments that no single theme can contain: Regular and Substantive Interaction, Community of Inquiry, data literacy, the Huron data governance engagement, and student agency. AI appears not as a node but as the substrate field behind everything, a faint purple glow that reshapes every element of the plan without being the center of any of them.

Student success sits at the bottom as the outcome toward which every theme converges. The faint teal lines from each theme to the outcome node represent the plan's central claim: the conditions that make learning relational are the same conditions that make it transformative.
